About us

Built at the bench

Dental technicians first

D.EIGHT DESIGN CENTER grew out of nearly five years of hands-on dental design work: first as a technician in a dental lab in Vietnam, then designing directly for clinics and labs in the United States and Australia.

Today D.EIGHT is a dedicated design team. Implant work is our core: single implant crowns, custom abutments, All-on-X bridges and bars. Around it we cover crown and bridge, removable and appliance design.

We only design. No milling, no printing, no shipping, so every hour goes into the file you send to your own mill, printer or production partner.

FAQ

Good to know

Before you send the first case

Can I try D.EIGHT before committing?
Yes. Your first two cases are free, up to US$50 in total, whatever the case type. Sign in to the customer portal and send your first case to start.
What do you need to start a case?
Intraoral or model scans (STL or PLY) and your Rx: tooth numbers, material, cement gap and any implant or library details. For surgical guides, add the DICOM/CBCT data.
How fast is delivery?
Designs are delivered within 24 hours of receiving a complete case. If a large full-arch or guide case needs more time, we confirm the timing when you send it.
How do revisions work?
Each case includes two free revisions before final export. Changes requested after approval are quoted by scope.
Do I need an account?
Just your Google account. Sign in to the customer portal to send cases, follow their status, approve designs and download files.
Do you mill, print or ship?
No. D.EIGHT is a design-only service. You receive production-ready STL files for your own mill, printer or production partner.
How is pricing set?
Per unit or per case, in USD. Use the form below to request our current price list or a quote for a specific case.
How do you handle patient data?
Files are handled confidentially and named by case ID. Please leave patient names out of case references and messages.
